Office workers are the only set drawn for an office: they sit and type at a desk. There are 4 workers in 1 art style.
Ninja Adventure has the most characters and the simplest licence, but the characters don't sit or type. Emote bubbles do most of the work.
LPC has proper sit, emote and fall-down animations, and each agent can look different. Every artist of the parts used has to be credited.
